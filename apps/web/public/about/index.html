About

ClockCover exists because one payroll accountant was cross-checking every missed clock entry for a whole company, alone, every month.

The problem we picked

Attendance systems already know who did not clock in. The missing piece is not detection — it is responsibility. Department managers can act on a gap the day it happens, but they never see it; the accountant sees all of them, weeks later, and has to chase each one. We route the signal to the person who can fix it, and involve payroll only when that person does not.

What we believe

  • One email a day beats a dashboard. Managers did not ask for another tool. They get a list of their own team's gaps and one tap to act. No account to remember.
  • The accountant should see less, not more. Only escalations reach payroll. A short list, not the full stream.
  • Vendor-agnostic on purpose. The pain is the same whatever system clocks the hours, so we read exports instead of building deep integrations — and build the connector ourselves when one is missing.
  • Boring about privacy. No biometrics, ever. Files are parsed and deleted. Every decision that touches personal data is written down before code is.

Where we are

Early access: the product runs end to end, the first employers are being onboarded by request, and every integration we build for them becomes part of the product. Pricing is per employer, by headcount, and public.
